📜 The Original Vision Origin Story
I would like to build a simple game that randomly races potatoes with little avatars on top of them. Each race should accommodate up to 12 contestants. Their names should be gathered before the race can begin. Each should be distinct and color coded. Once the contestants are finalized, a button should be made available to start the race. After it is pushed, the contestant avatars should be started on a racetrack of some sort (possibly left to right or a circle) and a countdown should commence. Once it hits zero, a big "Go!" graphic should appear and then the contestants' avatars should start racing on screen. The first one to do a lap or make it to the other side of the screen should be deemed the winner and their avatar should be displayed next to a trophy. On this display page, a reset button should appear. There should be a lot of randomization of their pace so that the winner is always random.
